# Google's guide on AI features and llms.txt

**Source:** https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/ai-optimization-guide · **Kind:** primary · **Accessed:** 2026-11-19

## What it says

- Google lists "LLMS.txt files and other special markup" among the things you can ignore for Google Search.
- It says creating such files for other services is fine, and does not affect visibility or rankings in Google Search.

## Quotes

> "Doing so will neither harm nor help your site's visibility or rankings in Google Search"

## Caveats

- It speaks about Google Search only. It says nothing about whether other tools or agents read the files.
