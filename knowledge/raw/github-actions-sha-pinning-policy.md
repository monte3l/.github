# The require-SHA-pinning policy

**Source:** https://docs.github.com/en/organizations/managing-organization-settings/disabling-or-limiting-github-actions-for-your-organization · **Kind:** primary · **Accessed:** 2026-11-19

## What it says

- When an organization turns on the policy that actions must be pinned to a full-length commit SHA, all actions must be. The same page says reusable workflows are not covered.

## Quotes

> "Reusable workflows can still be referenced by tag."

## Caveats

- The policy was introduced on 2025-08-15 (`https://github.blog/changelog/2025-08-15-github-actions-policy-now-supports-blocking-and-sha-pinning-actions/`), as reported by a research agent.
